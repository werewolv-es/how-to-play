# Pollygrapher

A witchcraft-using village investigator who sends their parrot Polly to read tarot cards from a target and their 4 nearest neighbours. Polly collects 2 cards per player from the target area, then introduces 2–3 errors from random players, and reports back in randomised parrot noises consistent within a game.
