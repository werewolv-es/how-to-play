# Novice Parrot Reader

A witchcraft-using village investigator whose pet parrot Polly reads tarot cards and reports them back as randomised parrot noises. Each night, selects two targets; Polly reads 2 cards from each plus 1 card from a random third player, returning 5 noises whose meanings are consistent but randomly assigned per game.
