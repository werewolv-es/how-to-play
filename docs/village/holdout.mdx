# Holdout

A village killing role that activates only in endgame (5 or fewer players alive). When triggered, may either agitate the village (forcing a double execution) or shoot a player with a penetrating kill.

This role has not yet been documented in the official How To Play guide. For more information, please refer to the [Unofficial H2P Wiki](https://unofficialhowtoplay.co.uk/index.php/Holdout). Note: the unofficial wiki may contain inaccurate or outdated content.
