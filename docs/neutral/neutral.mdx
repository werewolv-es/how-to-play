# Neutral Roles

The Neutral Roles are held by players who, either permanently or temporarily, are not aligned with any other faction of players having the same win condition.

:::info[Unofficial Wiki]
Additional information on this faction is available on the [Unofficial H2P Wiki](https://unofficialhowtoplay.co.uk/index.php/Neutrals). Note: the unofficial wiki may contain inaccurate or outdated content.
:::
