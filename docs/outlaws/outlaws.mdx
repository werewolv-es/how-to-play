# The Outlaws

The Outlaws are a neutral faction whose win condition is controlling at least half of all items in the game.

:::warning[Not Yet Documented]
This faction has not yet been documented in the official How To Play guide. For more information, please refer to the [Unofficial H2P Wiki](https://unofficialhowtoplay.co.uk/index.php/Outlaws). Note: the unofficial wiki may contain inaccurate or outdated content.
:::

## Roles

- [Bandit](/roles/Bandit) — steals all items from a target player each night
- [Highwayman](/roles/Highwayman) — has a single-use penetrating kill that bypasses standard protections
- [Outlaw Leader](/roles/OutlawLeader) — recruits village players into the Outlaws, but dies if they target an evil-aligned player
- [Relic Hunter](/roles/RelicHunter) — steals items from living or dead players, with an additional personal win condition of collecting five cursed artifacts
