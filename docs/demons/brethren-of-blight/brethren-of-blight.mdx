# Brethren of Blight

The Brethren of Blight are a Demons sub-faction that uses Blight and recruitment mechanics. Notably, they count toward village parity despite being evil-aligned.

:::warning[Not Yet Documented]
This faction has not yet been documented in the official How To Play guide. For more information, please refer to the [Unofficial H2P Wiki](https://unofficialhowtoplay.co.uk/index.php/Brethren_of_Blight). Note: the unofficial wiki may contain inaccurate or outdated content.
:::

## Roles

- [Brethren Shepherd](/roles/BrethrenShepherd) — recruits Damned players into the Brethren of Blight faction
- [Deacon](/roles/Deacon) — can detect Damned players or convert Brethren members into Fanatics
- [Fanatic](/roles/Fanatic) — an upgraded Brethren member that steals items and spreads incurable blight
- [Heretical Teacher](/roles/HereticalTeacher) — recruits up to two Damned players, but dies if they attempt to recruit a killing evil
