# The Demons

The Demons are a secretive faction, rumoured to exist. They are said to use a Blight mechanic — marking players with a curse before igniting them in a devastating kill that bypasses most defences.

:::warning[Not Yet Documented]
This faction has not yet been documented in the official How To Play guide. For more information, please refer to the [Unofficial H2P Wiki](https://unofficialhowtoplay.co.uk/index.php/Demons). Note: the unofficial wiki may contain inaccurate or outdated content.
:::

## Roles

- [Demon Lord](/roles/DemonLord) — blights targets and can ignite them in a kill that bypasses night immunity
- [Demon Prince](/roles/DemonPrince) — similar to the Demon Lord but can blight multiple targets before igniting
- [Hellhound](/roles/Hellhound) — reveals a target's role to themselves each night
- [Shadowcaster](/roles/Shadowcaster) — disguises players' alignments to Seers, with power scaling based on blight count
