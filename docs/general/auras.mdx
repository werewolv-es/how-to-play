# Auras in werewolv.es

Auras are status effects that can be applied to players, modifying their behaviour or abilities during the game. Some auras are applied by moderators at game start as part of the setup, while others are applied through in-game mechanics and items.

:::note
This page may not cover all auras available in the game.
:::

## Starting Auras

The following auras can be applied by moderators at game start:

<dl>
  <dt>Benign</dt>
  <dd>Gives the player a -1 modifier to their vote if they are voting for a village-aligned player.</dd>
  <dt>Blessed</dt>
  <dd>Blocks undead visitors.</dd>
  <dt>Clumsy</dt>
  <dd>Player is unable to activate items.</dd>
  <dt>Concussed</dt>
  <dd>Player has a chance to visit a neighbour of their target instead.</dd>
  <dt>Crippled</dt>
  <dd>Player is unable to visit more than 3 players away from them.</dd>
  <dt>CursedByFenrir</dt>
  <dd>Appears as a wolf to alignment checks and can be recruited by the Bloodmoon Cult.</dd>
  <dt>Damned</dt>
  <dd>Changes a player's win condition to align with any evil faction.</dd>
  <dt>Doomed</dt>
  <dd>Kills the player.</dd>
  <dt>Enduring</dt>
  <dd>Prevents any type of death or execution from occurring. Expires when used.</dd>
  <dt>Greedy</dt>
  <dd>Player is unable to pass items.</dd>
  <dt>HasAncestralLycanthropy</dt>
  <dd>Appears as a wolf to alignment checks and can be recruited by the Children of Acer.</dd>
  <dt>Incriminated</dt>
  <dd>Will appear as a killer and a witchcraft user to relevant checks.</dd>
  <dt>Inebriated</dt>
  <dd>Makes a player drunk.</dd>
  <dt>Isolated</dt>
  <dd>Removes a player from any night chats.</dd>
  <dt>Maimed</dt>
  <dd>Has a chance to kill the player when they visit others.</dd>
  <dt>Merciful</dt>
  <dd>Gives the player a -1 modifier to their vote if they are voting for a Villager, Sleepwalker, Insomniac, or a Lycan.</dd>
  <dt>Merry</dt>
  <dd>Makes a player behave the same as Drunk, and also has their speech altered.</dd>
  <dt>Persistent</dt>
  <dd>Gives the player a +1 modifier to their vote if they are voting for the same player they were voting for at the end of the previous day.</dd>
  <dt>Poisoned</dt>
  <dd>Lasts 3 nights. Each night, they have a chance of being role-blocked or dying.</dd>
  <dt>Righteous</dt>
  <dd>Gives the player a +1 modifier to their vote if they are voting for a player that is not village-aligned.</dd>
  <dt>Spellbound</dt>
  <dd>Makes the player do an additional visit to a random player each night.</dd>
  <dt>Tenacious</dt>
  <dd>The player is immune to role-block.</dd>
  <dt>Tough</dt>
  <dd>Prevents any type of death from occurring. Expires when used.</dd>
  <dt>Vigilant</dt>
  <dd>Items the player is holding are unable to be stolen.</dd>
  <dt>Vindictive</dt>
  <dd>Gives the player a +1 modifier to their vote if voting for someone that was voting for them at the end of the previous day.</dd>
  <dt>Wavering</dt>
  <dd>Gives the player a -1 modifier to their vote if they are voting for the same player they were voting for at the end of the previous day.</dd>
  <dt>Weakened</dt>
  <dd>Role-blocks the player.</dd>
</dl>

## Unknowing Variants

Several starting auras have 'Unknowing' versions that are invisible to the player holding them, while remaining visible to aura-detecting roles such as the Aura Seer. The auras with Unknowing variants are: Benign, Merciful, Persistent, Righteous, Vindictive, and Wavering.
