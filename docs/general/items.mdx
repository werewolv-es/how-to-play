# Items in werewolv.es

Items were an unannounced, surprise feature in ext-300. They have appeared in games since, but are not always included in a setup.

Items are either persistent or consumable. Consumable items disappear from the game once they have been used.

Items are either active or passive. Active items must be initiated by the Player that has control of them for them to work.

Passive and Consumable items such as Steel Armour or a Wolf Pelt will offer automatic protection from a single attack, but will be consumed and lost once an attack is blocked.

Items may be stolen by roles such as a thief, stolen by killing a player or stolen by using a Potion of Acquiescence.

Items may also be passed to a player during the night.

Items which have an active ability cannot be passed and used on the same night.

Items such as the potions and other targeted items count as visits and can be seen by other roles.

:::note
This page may not cover all items available in the game.
:::

## Active and Consumable

<dl>
  <dt>Arcanum</dt>
  <dd>
    Votes will be obscured the day after it is used - it will not be known who
    the votes are cast by.
  </dd>
  <dt>Arcurrum</dt>
  <dd>
    The day after use will be shorter than usual. (Only used in Speed Games)
  </dd>
  <dt>Bear Trap</dt>
  <dd>
    Sets a trap on a target; when someone visits that target, one visitor is
    randomly Crippled (unable to visit more than 3 players away). Can target
    self. Night immune targets are still trapped but protected from the
    role-block.
  </dd>
  <dt>Concitiorum</dt>
  <dd>
    The village will be forced two lynch two players the day after it is used.
  </dd>
  <dt>Crossbow</dt>
  <dd>
    Kills a player at night. Protection will stop the kill and a Huntsman will
    kill you and himself.
  </dd>
  <dt>Dagger of Paranoia</dt>
  <dd>
    Kills a random player who visits the holder at night. However, the item will
    still be consumed and lost even if no-one visits you.
  </dd>
  <dt>Desperate Shiv</dt>
  <dd>
    50% chance to kill the target, with a separate 50% chance to Cripple them.
    Single use. Counts as a visit and appears as a killer use.
  </dd>
  <dt>Enchanted Scroll</dt>
  <dd>
    A scroll created by the Spell Weaver. The recipient chooses one option from
    a set of effects, some beneficial and some harmful.
  </dd>
  <dt>Greater Arcanum</dt>
  <dd>
    Votes will be obscured the day after it is used - it will not be known who
    the votes are cast by, or for. Also removes the village option of a no
    lynch.
  </dd>
  <dt>Greater Potion of Incrimination</dt>
  <dd>Applies the Incriminated aura for the rest of the game.</dd>
  <dt>Heavy Shield</dt>
  <dd>
    If activated, it will protect the holder from a single night kill. However,
    when activated it will also role-block the holder.
  </dd>
  <dt>Inordinatum</dt>
  <dd>
    Swaps the identities of every living player with another random living
    player.
  </dd>
  <dt>Midnight Council Ballot</dt>
  <dd>
    Given to every living player to elect 3 Midnight Council members. Voting is
    hidden and does not register as a visit. Council members gain an aura and
    can be of any alignment.
  </dd>
  <dt>Minor Potion of Incrimination</dt>
  <dd>
    Applies Incriminated to the target for 1 night, causing them to appear as a
    killer and witchcraft user. Can target self. Cannot be generated by the
    Philosopher's Stone.
  </dd>
  <dt>Mystic Vault</dt>
  <dd>
    A shared item that cycles through the player list each night. One item can
    be taken from it per night, after which it auto-passes to the next player.
    Cannot be stolen or destroyed. Disappears when emptied.
  </dd>
  <dt>Poisoned Darts</dt>
  <dd>
    Poisons a player. When poisoned, a player has a 50% chance of being
    role-blocked and a 25% chance of dying. The poison lasts for 3 nights.
  </dd>
  <dt>Potion of Acquiescence</dt>
  <dd>Steals all of the items a player has.</dd>
  <dt>Potion of Charisma</dt>
  <dd>
    Doubles the Influence a target gains that night. Multiple potions used on
    the same target stack their multiplier.
  </dd>
  <dt>Potion of Clarity</dt>
  <dd>Finds out a player's role.</dd>
  <dt>Potion of Cleansing</dt>
  <dd>Removes a player's negative effects e.g. poison.</dd>
  <dt>Potion of Desolation</dt>
  <dd>Destroys all items the target holds. Single use. Counts as a visit.</dd>
  <dt>Potion of Destruction</dt>
  <dd>
    Destroys one random item the target holds. Single use. Counts as a visit.
  </dd>
  <dt>Potion of Incrimination</dt>
  <dd>
    Applies Incriminated to the target for 3 nights, causing them to appear as a
    killer, witchcraft user, and shifting their apparent alignment to the most
    populous evil faction. Cannot be generated by the Philosopher's Stone.
  </dd>
  <dt>Potion of Inebriation</dt>
  <dd>
    Makes a player drunk for the rest of the game, giving them a chance to fail
    their night action.
  </dd>
  <dt>Potion of Ineptitude</dt>
  <dd>
    Prevents the target from using active items for a single night. Passive
    items such armour and the disruption orb are unaffected.
  </dd>
  <dt>Potion of Insight</dt>
  <dd>
    Reveals the full role of a village-aligned target; returns a random village
    role if the target is not village-aligned. Single use. Counts as a visit.
  </dd>
  <dt>Potion of Invisibility</dt>
  <dd>Hides all visits the holder makes for one night. Single use.</dd>
  <dt>Potion of Poison</dt>
  <dd>
    Applies the Poisoned aura for three nights. Each night, there is a 25%
    chance of killing the target and a separate 50% chance to role-block them.
  </dd>
  <dt>Potion of Pollymorph</dt>
  <dd>Turns a player into a parrot for one day.</dd>
  <dt>Potion of Protection</dt>
  <dd>
    Protects a target from a preventable kill for one night. Neither the holder
    nor the target is notified if a kill was blocked.
  </dd>
  <dt>Potion of Tenacity</dt>
  <dd>Grants a player immunity from role-blocks for a single night.</dd>
  <dt>Potion of Toughness</dt>
  <dd>
    Permanently applies the Tough aura to a target. The target receives no
    notification.
  </dd>
  <dt>Potion of Transference</dt>
  <dd>
    Swaps the holder's avatar with a target player's. Both players are notified
    of the swap.
  </dd>
  <dt>Potion of Restoration</dt>
  <dd>
    Recharges a player's single use abilities e.g Revivers, Masons, Witches etc.
  </dd>
  <dt>Potion of Transmutation</dt>
  <dd>
    Gives a player a new role from within their faction. Does not affect players
    with night immunity.
  </dd>
  <dt>Potion of Vulnerability</dt>
  <dd>Removes a player's night immunity the night after it is used.</dd>
  <dt>Potion of Weakness</dt>
  <dd>Role-blocks a player the night of use.</dd>
  <dt>Minor Potion of Clarity</dt>
  <dd>Finds out a player's alignment.</dd>
  <dt>Sanguine Horn</dt>
  <dd>
    Triggers a Blood Moon which will afflict approximately 25% of the village
    with lycanthropy.
  </dd>
  <dt>Rusty Crossbow</dt>
  <dd>A crossbow with a 50% chance of backfiring and killing the user.</dd>
  <dt>Tumultum</dt>
  <dd>
    Sounds a horn during the night which makes every player randomly visit
    someone else, and be able to see anyone who visits them.
  </dd>
  <dt>War Spear</dt>
  <dd>
    Kills a player at night. However, it will also role-block you. Protection
    will stop the kill and a Huntsman will kill you and himself.
  </dd>
</dl>

## Active and Persistent

<dl>
  <dt>Demonic Eye</dt>
  <dd>
    Shows the holder who is blighted. It also allows them to remove blight from
    one player a night.
  </dd>
  <dt>Fenrir's Maw</dt>
  <dd>
    The Wolfpack deity item, held by the Wolfpack player with the most
    Influence. Used during Deity battles in Kingdom games.
  </dd>
  <dt>Gauntlet of Blight</dt>
  <dd>Used by the holder to blight one person per night.</dd>
  <dt>Hammer of Desolation</dt>
  <dd>
    Destroys all items the target holds each night. Cannot destroy the Heart of
    Fenrir. Cannot be item-blocked, though the ability can be role-blocked.
    Affects night immune targets.
  </dd>
  <dt>Hammer of Destruction</dt>
  <dd>
    Destroys one random item the target holds each night. Cannot destroy the
    Heart of Fenrir. Does not register as a visit.
  </dd>
  <dt>Heart of Hades</dt>
  <dd>Used to kill all of the players who have been blighted.</dd>
  <dt>Hell Helm</dt>
  <dd>Shows the holder all the messages a player sends during the night.</dd>
  <dt>Holy Lights</dt>
  <dd>
    These must be passed to a different player every night. They will light up
    if everyone holding a light is Village-aligned.
  </dd>
  <dt>Loki's Sceptre</dt>
  <dd>
    The Coven deity item, held by the Coven player with the most Influence. Used
    during Deity battles in Kingdom games.
  </dd>
  <dt>Manacles of Spite</dt>
  <dd>
    Allows the holder to role-block a player every night. However, it also
    role-blocks the holder when it is used.
  </dd>
  <dt>Martyr's Crown</dt>
  <dd>
    Redirects a kill from the target to the holder, killing the holder.
    Protection is unable to block the kill.
  </dd>
  <dt>Mercurial Caduceus</dt>
  <dd>
    Sets three word substitutions that apply automatically to a target's
    messages for the rest of the game. The target gains the Corrected aura.
  </dd>
  <dt>Obelisk of Blight</dt>
  <dd>
    Accumulates demonic energy: 1 point per blighted player, 2 points per
    Brethren member, 6 points for worshipping (role-blocks the Brethren
    Shepherd), and 12 points if a blighted player is executed. When fully
    charged, grants the Sceptre of Darkness holder the Shadowcaster role, or the
    Heart of Hades holder the Demon Prince role.
  </dd>
  <dt>Sceptre of Darkness</dt>
  <dd>
    Allows the holder to make a target person appear as a Demon for the rest of
    the game.
  </dd>
  <dt>Serpentine Talisman</dt>
  <dd>
    Summons snakes to a target's house, arriving the following night. Each
    night, one random player from the target and their visitors is poisoned. The
    swarm moves ±1 house each night. The holder is immune to their own snakes.
    Counts as a visit.
  </dd>
  <dt>Staff of Bedazzlement</dt>
  <dd>
    Makes the target Spellbound for the rest of the game (causing them to make
    an extra random visit each night) and causes them to appear as a witchcraft
    user. The effect is visible and removable by a Priestess or Potion of
    Cleansing. The target receives no notification.
  </dd>
  <dt>Tarot Cards</dt>
  <dd>
    Each night, check a player and receive one of their tarot cards showing
    their alignment, killer, or witchcraft status. One of the two cards returned
    is from a different random player.
  </dd>
  <dt>Thief's Cowl</dt>
  <dd>
    Steals all items from a target player. Role-blocks the user when activated.
  </dd>
  <dt>Tyr's Shield</dt>
  <dd>
    The Village deity item, held by the Village player with the most Influence.
    Used during Deity battles in Kingdom games.
  </dd>
  <dt>Vermin Talisman</dt>
  <dd>
    Summons a rat swarm to a target's house, arriving the following night.
    Affected players are role-blocked, item-blocked, and seen visiting a random
    neighbour. The swarm moves ±2 houses each night. Can be dispersed and
    redeployed.
  </dd>
  <dt>War Axe</dt>
  <dd>
    33% chance to kill the target, with a separate 66% chance to Maim them
    (visiting while Maimed has escalating kill risk). Activates each night.
    Counts as a visit. The holder appears as a killer.
  </dd>
  <dt>War Hammer</dt>
  <dd>
    20% chance to kill the target, with a separate 75% chance to Concuss them
    (causing them to visit a random neighbour instead of their intended target).
    Activates each night. Counts as a visit. The holder appears as a killer.
  </dd>
  <dt>Whispering Orb</dt>
  <dd>
    An anonymous night chat shared between all Orb holders. Can be toggled on or
    off each night. Messages are received regardless of toggle state.
  </dd>
</dl>

## Passive and Consumable

<dl>
  <dt>Altruistic Trinket</dt>
  <dd>
    Forces the holder to automatically pass it and all held items each night.
    The holder is Clumsy and cannot activate items. The Greedy aura overrides
    this effect.
  </dd>
  <dt>Ankh of Reincarnation</dt>
  <dd>
    If you are killed at night whilst holding the Ankh, you will be revived a
    day later.
  </dd>
  <dt>Ankh of Retribution</dt>
  <dd>
    If you are killed at night whilst holding the Ankh, the player who killed
    you will be murdered the following night. However, protection is able to
    block the attack.
  </dd>
  <dt>Ankh of Possession</dt>
  <dd>
    If you are killed at night whilst holding the Ankh, you will swap bodies
    with your killer the following night.
  </dd>
  <dt>Heart of Fenrir</dt>
  <dd>
    Gives the holder night immunity. However, if all the wolves are dead, it
    turns the holder into an Omegawolf.
  </dd>
  <dt>Iron Collar</dt>
  <dd>Protects the holder from a single lynch.</dd>
  <dt>Plot Armour</dt>
  <dd>
    Protects the holder from a single night kill (used at Mod's discretion).
  </dd>
  <dt>Rusty Armour</dt>
  <dd>Has a 50% chance to protect the holder from a single night kill.</dd>
  <dt>Steel Armour</dt>
  <dd>Protects the holder from a single night kill.</dd>
  <dt>Warg's Bracer</dt>
  <dd>
    If the holder is killed by the Wolfpack, the holder will become a wolf
    (similar to the Bloodmoon Cult).
  </dd>
  <dt>Wolf Pelt</dt>
  <dd>
    Protects the holder from a single night kill, but it will make them look
    like a wolf to the Seer.
  </dd>
</dl>

## Runes

Runes are active and persistent items, they do not disappear from the game when they are used.

Once a rune is used, it needs to be recharged and will only be available for use by the user three nights later.

However, if the holder passes a used rune to another player, it will be usable again immediately.

All runes make the holder appear to use Witchcraft
